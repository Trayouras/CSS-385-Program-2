Tank Z is a zombie survival tank single player rogue like game. In it, you control a tank, avoiding enemies and shooting them to survive waves of undead You will collect upgrades to last longer against the zombies, vampires, skeletons, ghosts, and other undead you might recognize if you look them up on Google Like the early resident evil games this has tank controls and zombies, but unlike those games it plays entirely differently. This is a top down wave shooter like vampire survivors, if they were cool and had a tank if you’ve ever wondered why the military always loses in zombie movies this is the game for you. This won’t answer the question but it is fun and you’ll enjoy it.

This game is based on controlling a tank and surviving against endless (or a very large number for now) waves of enemies. Your tank has tank controls, meaning you cannot strafe and have to make somewhat slow and deliberate turns. It has one weapon, which I currently intend to only allow to fire forward. It fires a projectile that explodes in an area, pushing enemies and dealing damage. This should work with the turning to create the primary challenge of the game; destroying enemies while staying a safe distance from them, as you also cannot drive in reverse. Every wave you will be able to upgrade your tank with a choice between three randomly chosen upgrades. Enemies, based on the type evidenced by their model, will have varying levels of speed, health, and damage output, and may have special attributes or abilities. More difficult enemies will spawn in greater numbers as the waves progress. The objective of the game is to survive as many waves as possible by destroying all the enemies that spawn as part of the wave. 

Currently, the prototype has the basic movement, shooting, and enemies. Visuals will be reworked, sound will be properly implemented, and elements to facilitate waves and progression will be added in later iterations.
